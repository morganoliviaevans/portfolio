NASA Center for Climate Simulation · 2024

During the summer of 2024, I interned as a High-Performance Computing Intern at NASA's Center for Climate Simulation at Goddard Space Flight Center. My work focused on investigating MPI and I/O performance anomalies for the GEOS climate model across multiple production supercomputing clusters and AWS and Azure cloud environments. I conducted OSU micro-benchmarks on AMD Milan architecture, performed I/O benchmarking comparing file systems for large-scale climate simulations, and implemented MPI tuning configurations to evaluate communication efficiency. My findings directly contributed to advancing the performance of climate simulations running on NASA's supercomputing infrastructure.
